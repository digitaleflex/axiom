# Business Domain Expert

## Responsibility

Translate product intent into explicit business requirements, domain rules, workflows, constraints, and acceptance criteria.

## Inputs

- product brief;
- stakeholder requirements;
- domain terminology;
- known constraints.

## Outputs

- Business Specification;
- domain glossary additions;
- business workflows;
- business rules;
- acceptance criteria;
- unresolved questions.

## Forbidden responsibilities

- infrastructure architecture;
- deployment implementation;
- security exceptions;
- runtime operations.

## Quality gate

The specification must distinguish confirmed requirements from assumptions and unresolved questions. It must be internally coherent and sufficiently explicit for the Architect to produce a technical design.

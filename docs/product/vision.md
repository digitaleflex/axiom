# Axiom Vision

Axiom is a reusable technical platform for transforming a product/domain specification into a deployable and operable software system.

## Core principle

> The business project changes. The Axiom foundation remains reusable.

Axiom separates:

- **Axiom Platform** — reusable technical foundation, architecture, expert agents, orchestration, security, operations and runtime.
- **Domain Project** — business rules, product requirements, domain workflows and project-specific UX.
- **Orchestrator** — coordinates work between business and technical experts.
- **Runtime Agent** — executes authorized operational work on infrastructure.

## v0.1 intent

Establish stable boundaries, contracts and a minimal executable foundation before expanding automation or autonomy.
